# Source and aggregate-data rights

Only aggregate feasibility statistics, original audit annotations and source-link/checksum manifests are distributed. There are no individual sample records, third-party source PDFs, consumption microdata or copied abstracts. The contributors license only their original contributions to these summaries under CC BY 4.0, to the extent they hold the relevant rights; this does not override underlying source terms or confer rights in third-party material.

- FAOSTAT: consult the linked FAO dataset and its CC BY/database terms. National feed-use totals do not identify rations or origin-specific dose.
- EFSA VMPR/DCF: consult the linked records and CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements.
- EFSA pesticide monitoring: retain EFSA/record-specific copyright and usage terms. Open availability is not blanket redistribution permission.
- FAO/JMPR, Codex, OECD and journal articles: links/checksums and original critical annotations only; obtain full text from the rights holder.

The source registry contains the original bounded audit's recorded terms, not a legal guarantee that a later download has identical licensing. Recheck the official source before redistribution. When rights are uncertain, obtain the data independently rather than copying them from a private archive into this repository.

Frozen protocols are historical specifications. Later source reconciliation and methods notes explain what actually happened; do not treat planned blinded assessments or historical wording as completed procedures. Data harmonisation outputs retain original labels such as `quantified_rows` for compatibility; those flags include numeric-bearing LOQ-coded observations and must not be treated as validated uncensored concentrations.
